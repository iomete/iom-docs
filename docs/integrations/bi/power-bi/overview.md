## Choose a Connection Method

IOMETE supports three ways to connect Power BI. Choose the guide that matches the connector and protocol you want to use.

| Method | Connector | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Arrow Flight (recommended)** | IOMETE Arrow Flight ODBC connector | [Arrow Flight](./arrow-flight.md) |
| Thrift | Power BI's built-in Spark connector | [Thrift](./thrift.md) |
| Thrift ODBC | Simba Spark ODBC driver | [Thrift ODBC](./odbc.md) |
