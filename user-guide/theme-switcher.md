Set the Console to a light or dark appearance that works for your workspace. Select **System Preferences** to follow your operating system's appearance.

:::info Version Availability
The theme switcher is available in IOMETE v4.
:::

## Changing the Theme

Switch themes from any Console page that displays the app sidebar.

1. Expand the sidebar if it is collapsed.
2. Select your avatar and name at the top of the sidebar to open the user menu.
3. Open **Theme**.
4. Choose one:
   - **System Preferences**: Matches your operating system's light or dark appearance.
   - **Light**: Keeps the Console in light mode.
   - **Dark**: Keeps the Console in dark mode.

The Console switches themes immediately. A checkmark identifies your active choice, so you don't need to save or reload the page.

<Img src="/img/user-guide/theme-switcher/theme-menu.png" alt="User menu with the Theme submenu showing System Preferences, Light, and Dark" maxWidth="518px" />

The [SQL Editor's color scheme](./sql-editor/query-results.md#customizing-editor-appearance) also uses the light or dark variant that matches the Console theme.

## Using System Preferences

Use **System Preferences** to keep the Console consistent with the rest of your device. If your operating system switches between light and dark modes, the Console updates automatically.

If you select **Light** or **Dark**, changes to your operating system theme don't affect the Console.

## Keeping Your Theme Preference

Your browser remembers the selected theme between visits, even after you reload or sign out. Other open Console tabs in the same browser update to match.

:::info
The theme isn't part of your IOMETE account settings. Select it again when you use another browser, browser profile, or device. People who share a browser profile also share its saved theme.
:::

:::note
If your account doesn't belong to a domain, the **Available domains** page uses the light theme and saves **Light** as your current selection.
:::
